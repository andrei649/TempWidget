TempWidget privacy policy
=========================
Last updated: 16 November 2026

TempWidget reads hardware sensors on your PC (temperatures, fan speeds, load,
power, memory and battery values, and hardware model names) and displays them
on your screen.

- TempWidget does not collect, store or transmit any personal information.
- TempWidget makes no network connections. There is no telemetry, no
  analytics, no advertising and no account.
- Your settings and an error log are kept in a local folder on your PC
  (%APPDATA%\TempWidget). Nothing leaves your PC.
- If you turn on logging, readings are written to CSV files in the same
  local folder. They stay on your PC.
- The "Copy sensor report" button puts a list of hardware model names and
  sensor values on your clipboard. It is only shared if you paste it somewhere
  yourself.

Third-party software: CPU sensors are read through the PawnIO driver
(https://pawnio.eu). Its official installer is included with TempWidget and is
run only when you choose full sensors; nothing is downloaded. PawnIO has its
own licence and can be removed separately in Windows Settings.
